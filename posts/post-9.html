Surgery for Diabetes- Lets talk about Cure and not control

Diabetes November 27, 2013 Dr. Atul Peters

Embracing Change: The Need for Surgery for Diabetes in India

Last evening I was at the south Delhi DMA meet with more than 200 specialist and physicians. It was a splendid moment sharing my view about current trends in Metabolic treatment for Diabetes. To my surprise many of my colleagues still are unaware of the surgery for diabetes in India!! The governing body of the world for diabetes called the IDF or the International Diabetes Federation has set up clear guidelines for prioritization for surgery in diabetics, just that we are still resisting the change and still don’t seem to offer treatment to our diabetics. My appeal to all my friends and colleagues— Diabetes is now a curable surgical disease—Please consider it as a treatment option for yourself and your patients rather than living with diabetes and its complications…
Lets get rid of it …Now..
