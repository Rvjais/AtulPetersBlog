Embracing Contentment and Gratitude: My Journey to Melbourne

Happiness cannot be traveled to, owned, earned, worn, or consumed. Happiness is the spiritual experience of living every minute with love, grace, and gratitude.” Denis Waitley

The recent days have been very busy; they were filled with lot of contentment and with a sense of satisfaction.

The initial spark that made me change my base to Melbourne was the upcoming DAA conference in May 2016. Then I planned to submit an abstract for the Oral Paper Presentation, which got selected and I finally packed my bags and came to this beautiful country!

Explore more about finding happiness and meaningful experiences on our blog. Join us as we share stories and insights to inspire a life filled with contentment.
