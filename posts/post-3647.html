Understanding RTP: A Core Element of Game Selection

Return to Player (RTP) is a critical metric for assessing the profitability of a game. It indicates the percentage of wagered money that a game is expected to pay back to players over time. At Virginbet Casino, RTPs are generally competitive, often exceeding the industry average of around 95%. Here’s why RTP matters:

  • Higher RTP means better odds: Games with an RTP of 96% or higher are considered excellent choices for players seeking value.
  • Long-term profitability: While RTP doesn’t guarantee a win, higher percentages suggest more favourable outcomes over extended play.
  • Game variety: Ensure to check the RTP of various games, from slots to table games, as they can vary significantly.
